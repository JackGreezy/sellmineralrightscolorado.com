# Sell Mineral Rights Colorado — Site-Specific Rules

This project must have a unique business voice and a unique visual interpretation of its reference. Do not import a reusable mineral-rights voice pack or reuse the ending modules, slug treatment, FAQ treatment, related-content treatment, or CTA treatment from another fleet site.

## Voice lock

The voice is the Colorado tract economics brief: calm, precise, and numbers-first. It explains ownership through the tract schedule, paid decimal, production ledger, decline evidence, lease burdens, split-estate record, nearby activity, written offer scope, and closing schedule. It never uses first-person singular business narration. It never promises a highest offer, instant value, guaranteed closing, or a proprietary formula.

## Visual lock

The reference is Swisslog. Retain its wide industrial imagery, Nexa for Swisslog typography, sharp red/black/white system, long horizontal editorial measure, asymmetric split compositions, expertise cadence, horizontal story rail, rectangular controls, and dense red footer. Slug pages use a single wide hero image, broad readable article measure, Colorado inventory-line FAQ, conveyor-style related links, and a red closing panel. Do not insert rounded generic card stacks.

## Release blockers

- No visible street address. The address may exist only inside a Google Maps embed query.
- No footer thumbnails, decorative mini-images, one-pixel images, floating image glitches, emoji, or Unicode-arrow decoration.
- Exactly one image on every slug page: the hero image.
- No vertically stacked or one-character-wide desktop text.
- No invisible body text, low-contrast copy, clipped sections, overlapping header items, dead carousel arrows, inert hamburger, or unreachable dropdown links.
- Desktop header must show real routes, the phone number, and a clear contact CTA.
- Contact form fields must be exactly Name, Email, Phone, and Message.
- Footer must contain a Google Maps embed on every route.
- Every page must use the Swisslog-derived font and visual system.
- Homepage hero must contain an SEO-driven H1.
- All written content must be business voice, never first-person singular narration.

The build is not complete until both machine gates pass, the full desktop/mobile screenshot set is reviewed, interactions are exercised, production is deployed, and the live alias is verified.
